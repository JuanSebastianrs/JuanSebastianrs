## Research focus: Computer Vision for Sports

Tactical Vision connects detection and temporal tracking with geometric reconstruction and evaluation. The project includes RF-DETR, YOLO, ByteTrack, Viterbi-based ball tracking and training infrastructure on Google Cloud Vertex AI.

The [published evaluation report](https://github.com/JuanSebastianrs/VM_FOOTBALL/blob/main/results_final/EVALUATION_METHODOLOGY.md) reports **MOTA 88.78%** and **HOTA 58.71%** for active-player tracking. HOTA is averaged over 19 IoU thresholds; metric scope and protocol matter when comparing systems.
